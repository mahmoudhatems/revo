# REVO — Sources and Credits

Team REVO · NASA Space Apps Challenge, Cairo 2026 · Mahmoud Hatem, Nourhan Mahmoud, Ahmed Saad, Mohammed Osama, Hanya ElMasry.

## NASA resources (design references and calibration)
- Mars Trek (JPL) — https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/ — Jezero terrain character, crater/dune layout, elevation layering (surface tiles, hub layout).
- MEDA, Perseverance (JPL) — https://mars.nasa.gov/mars2020/weather/ — dust opacity, pressure, wind at Jezero (storm timing, solar output).
- InSight weather archive (JPL) — https://mars.nasa.gov/insight/weather/ — seasonal cross-check of dust/pressure.
- DONKI (NASA CCMC) — https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/ — flare/CME classification (Sol 21 solar particle event).
- MSL RAD, Curiosity (JPL) — https://mars.nasa.gov/msl/spacecraft/instruments/rad/ — surface dose ranges (radiation meter).
- MER Opportunity archive (NASA PDS) — https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mer/ — 2018 dust-storm timeline (Sol 11 crisis, emergency ending).
- NASA image library — https://images.nasa.gov/ — visual reference for suit/habitat/rover proportions.
